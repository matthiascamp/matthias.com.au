04 How a software project runs

The process.

Four steps, no surprises. You know the scope and the cost before I write anything.

  1. 01 Step one

    Discovery call

    You walk me through your business and what you need. No obligation — just a clear picture of the problem first.

  2. 02 Step two

    Scope & MVP

    I propose a scope and MVP — the smallest version that delivers real value — so we agree before a line of code is written.

  3. 03 Step three

    Contract & deposit

    A plain-English contract covers deliverables, timeline and payment terms. A deposit secures your place in the schedule.

  4. 04 Go live

    Build & delivery

    I build to the agreed spec, keeping you informed throughout. You review, we adjust, we go live — ongoing support from there.
