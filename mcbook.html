What it does

Built around
the booking.

Six things that together handle the whole cycle — from a customer booking in to knowing the history when they return.

01

Online booking

A simple booking flow customers can use themselves, without needing to call during business hours. Available around the clock.

02

Managed calendar

See and manage every booking in one place. Set your availability, block time, and handle changes — all from one clear view.

03

Customer records

Contact details and booking history kept together, so you always have context when someone comes back.

04

Reminders

Automated reminders help reduce missed appointments and last-minute gaps — sent without any manual effort.

Your availability

Set the hours, services and slots that make sense for how you actually work. Customers only see what's actually free.

In short

The problem,
and the fix.

Bookings taken by phone, paper or messages are easy to double-book, forget, or lose. MCBook gives customers a way to book themselves in while you keep one clear view of everything that's coming up.

Problem

Bookings by phone, paper or messages — easy to double-book, forget or lose track of.

Solution built

Online bookings, a managed calendar, customer records and reminders in one system.

Approach

Web-based and quick to set up — easy for non-technical staff to run day to day.

Benefit

Less admin, fewer missed bookings, and a clearer picture of what's coming up.
