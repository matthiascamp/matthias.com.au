How YieldPOS started

The story behind
YieldPOS.

I developed YieldPOS after noticing that Crisp on Creek's point-of-sale system was outdated. I proposed replacing it with a new system designed around the shop's needs, then built and implemented it.

YieldPOS is now available for use at other businesses.
