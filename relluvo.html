The idea

A customer relationship is more than a contact card.

An enquiry becomes a quote. The quote becomes an order. Then come supplier messages, files, deliveries, tasks and invoices. Relluvo gives each piece a place under the same job so the next person can see what has happened and what needs doing.

Inside the workspace

The useful details stay connected.

Start with the core job record. Turn on the parts your business actually uses.

01 / PEOPLE

Know who is involved.

Customer and supplier contacts, with their role, details and related jobs. Keep several people at one organisation without losing the thread.

02 / WORK

Give every job a next step.

Track a job’s stage, owner, notes and due tasks. A simple board shows what is to do, moving, blocked or complete.

03 / FILES

File the source material.

Keep quotes, purchase orders, shipment notices and other documents with the job they belong to.

04 / STOCK

Follow goods through.

Record partial receipts, batches, expiry dates, dispatches and invoice allocations. Review supplier shipment data before it changes stock.

05 / ACCOUNTING

Hand off to Xero.

Create and check linked draft quotes, invoices and supplier purchase orders in a Xero demo company. Live accounting rules are mapped during setup.

06 / PROCESS

Make reminders useful.

Add optional checkpoints for documents, reviews or approvals. The prompts can follow your real process once the steps are agreed.

One job, one story

From first message to finished work.

  1. 01Capture the enquiry

    Review the email and choose the right customer job.

  2. 02Move the work

    Keep the quote, orders, files and team tasks together.

  3. 03Receive and deliver

    Check incoming goods, then record what has shipped.

  4. 04Close the loop

    Prepare the invoice and review the linked Xero draft.

The email and Xero connectors are in pilot testing. A production setup needs your own mailbox, accounts, approvals and stock rules checked with your team.
