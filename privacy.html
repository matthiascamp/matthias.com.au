Privacy

Privacy.

How I handle the information you share through this site. Last updated July 2026.

This is a small, independent business run by Matthias Campbell (ABN 19 469 850 250) in Brisbane. I keep data collection to the minimum needed to reply to you and do the work — nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

What I collect

  • Details you send through the contact form — your name, email, optional phone number, chosen enquiry type, and message.
  • Anything you include when you email or call me directly.
  • Standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) kept by the services that host this site and handle the form.

How I use it

Only to respond to your enquiry, provide the service you've asked about, and keep in touch about that work. If you become a client, I'll use your details to deliver and support the agreed work.

Services I rely on

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the contact form is delivered to me through Formspree. When you submit the form, your message passes through Formspree so it can reach my inbox. Each of these providers handles data under its own privacy terms.

Keeping it and removing it

I keep enquiry and client information only as long as it's useful for our dealings or required for tax and record-keeping. You can ask me to see what I hold about you, correct it, or delete it — just get in touch.

Contact

For anything about privacy, email hello@matthias.com.au or call 0424 106 527. This page is a plain-language summary of how I work, not legal advice.
