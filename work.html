Point of Sale · Retail

YieldPOS at Crisp on Creek

Crisp on Creek is a fruit-and-veg shop in Mt Gravatt, Brisbane. Their register ran on an ageing system that was increasingly hard to work with. I built YieldPOS — a Windows point-of-sale designed for fresh retail — to replace it, and it runs in the shop day to day.

  • ProblemAn old register system that was dated and awkward for fresh-food retail.
  • BuiltA touchscreen register plus back-office admin — weighed produce, barcode scanning, receipts, cash drawer and a customer display.
  • ApproachAn Electron desktop app with an offline-first local database, so it keeps selling even without internet, with optional cloud sync.
  • BenefitA faster, staff-friendly checkout the shop owns outright — no subscription and no reliance on a live connection.
