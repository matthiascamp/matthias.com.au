Questions

asked.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and any custom features. I give a quote once we've discussed what you need.

Do I need a website before starting SEO?

Yes. If you need one built, we can discuss both together.

What counts as a content piece?

An article, case study or substantial page update chosen around your customers' needs. New content starts with Grow; Maintain focuses on technical upkeep.

Can I change plans?

Yes, move up or down any time with 30 days' notice.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No. I do the work properly and report honestly on the results.

What happens if I cancel?

30 days' notice. You keep everything — content, reports and any changes made to your site.

What if I'm not in Brisbane?

The work is done remotely, so location doesn't matter for SEO. Website projects can also be done remotely.
