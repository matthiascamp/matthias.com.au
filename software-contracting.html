How it works

Simple to start.
Clear all the way.

Start with the problem

One developer, from first call to delivery.

Tell me what is slowing you down. I will help decide whether custom software is the right answer, then agree a scope and cost before any build begins.

  1. Talk it through. We identify the workflow, who uses it and what needs to change.
  2. Choose the right approach. If an existing product solves it better, I will say so.
  3. Agree the scope. You know what will be built and what it will cost before work starts.
  4. Build in stages. You can see progress and give feedback as each useful part takes shape.
  5. Keep one point of contact. You deal directly with the person doing the work.

Projects can be as small as a single automation or as broad as a business system. The first conversation is about whether the work is worthwhile and what a sensible next step would be.
